Features

All features are included in the extension. There are no accounts, subscriptions or license keys.

Detect

Snagly watches the page you are on for supported video and audio files and HLS (.m3u8) and DASH (.mpd) streams. You see resolution, codec, bitrate and size, can filter and sort the list, and can paste a media URL yourself.

Download

  • Save as MP4 (H.264/AAC where supported); processing runs in your browser.
  • Choose quality and fallback options for bulk jobs.
  • Pause, resume and reorder queued downloads.
  • Schedule downloads for a set time or for when the computer is idle.
  • Record supported live HLS and DASH streams.
  • Use custom file naming rules and a speed limit.

Trim, crop and audio

  • Trim by start and end timestamps.
  • Crop to original, 16:9 or vertical 9:16 and adjust the position.
  • Remove the audio track, extract audio only, or combine separate video and audio tracks.

Subtitles

Download supported subtitle tracks (SRT, VTT, ASS, TTML where available), translate them, and burn them into the video. Translation sends the caption text to third-party services; see the privacy policy.

Private vault

  • Saved files live in local extension storage on your device.
  • Folders, tags and search.
  • PIN lock with automatic locking, a decoy PIN and a panic-hide shortcut (Alt+Shift+H).
  • Optional temporary storage that deletes files after a timer.
  • Export files to your computer.

Send to phone

Open a saved video, show a QR code, and scan it with your phone camera. Transfers use a direct browser-to-browser connection set up through a receiver page on this site.

How it works

  1. Open a page with a video and click the Snagly icon.
  2. Pick a quality, or select Trim, Audio, Subtitles or Vault.
  3. Click Download. The file saves to your computer or to the vault.

What it will not do

Snagly does not remove DRM, bypass paywalls or logins, or work on protected streaming services. Some sites are excluded or unsupported, and results depend on the site and your browser.
