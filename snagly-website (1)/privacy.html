Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. This policy covers the Snagly Video Downloader Chrome extension and this website.

Summary: Snagly works locally in your browser. We have no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we do not sell data. Two optional features contact other services, explained below.

Stored on your device

Settings, naming rules, bookmarks, folders, tags, queue and history, vault files and metadata, and PIN hashes stay in your browser's local storage. Uninstalling the extension or clearing data from Settings removes them.

Features that use other services

FeatureWhat is sent and where
Subtitle translation (only when you choose a language)The subtitle text is sent to Google Translate or MyMemory to be translated. Those services have their own privacy policies.
Send to phoneYour phone opens a receiver page on this website. For a direct link, the link contains the media address and file name, so this site's host may see them in its request logs. For a peer-to-peer transfer, a signaling service (PeerJS) and public STUN servers (Google, Cloudflare) help the two devices connect; the video itself travels between your devices.
Fetching media you downloadSnagly requests files directly from the website that serves the video, as your browser would.

Optional error log

If you turn on error logging in Settings, a short error type and the extension version are saved in local browser storage (last 30 entries). Nothing is sent to us. You can clear it any time.

Snagly does not run remote code. All code ships inside the extension.

Your choices

You can delete vault items, set a temporary-file timer, export or reset settings, or uninstall the extension to remove all local data.

Children

Snagly is not directed at children under 13.
